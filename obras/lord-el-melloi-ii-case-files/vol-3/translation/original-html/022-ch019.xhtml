“Reines, focus your Mystic Eyes.”

Right now, however, that was something to be thankful for.

Due to that overreaction, the girl’s precision in magecraft had become prominent.

With each touch from Islo, the dress was reconstructed.

With each word from Maio, the concentration of various substances within the blood and the presence of neurotransmitters was altered, bringing it back to life.

And when those things had reached a perfect harmony, the girl activated the ritual, calling out in a loud voice.
