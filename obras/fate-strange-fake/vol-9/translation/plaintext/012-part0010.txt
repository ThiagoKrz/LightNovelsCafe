Afterword

Welcome back, it’s Narita again! Sorry for the long wait! Last year, the 20th anniversary of my writing career, was a tumultuous year, full of work related to adaptations of my series and all other sorts of movement.

As FsF also approaches its conclusion, I gained more momentum and energy, and after I made a few teams drop out, I, considerably out of my mind, thought “It’d be better if all of this was happening simultaneously in the last volume instead of volume 9” and rewrote things into the current shape. I can’t even call this A rewrite, it was THE rewrite. Trusting the power of my heart, I ran through volume 9, and made this “incoming climax” into the cut-off point, with every team at the opening of their final battle—Only one volume left until the curtains are closed on the Fake Holy Grail War! (No idea what the page count on the final volume will look like)

I’ll do my best to deliver the best possible finale for the readers who came this far…!

Needless to say, I consulted Nasu about the transformation of you-know-which-character this volume!

Me: “That will be the twist. What is he like having lost the power of the gods and made into a pure-blooded human?”

Nasu: “An infallibly affable man, but without any of the kindness you’d expect from someone like that. If he can’t find a need for a [REDACTED FOR SPOILERS], he’d blow up Kayneth’s building with the most angelic smile.”

Me: “What? I need to reconstruct his relationship to his Master with a personality like this!? You know what, challenge accepted!”

And that’s what drove me to write the character like that. The result was well, that daunting closing scene that wasn’t anywhere in the original plans!

Nasu: “I feel like the deaged character is going to add like 50 extra pages to the final volume. Are you sure that’s a good idea?”

Me: “…I…Ah…CHALLENGE ACCEPTED!” Nasu: “I’m sure feeling nothing foreboding from your display of determination. Nuh-uh… nothing you said there will help loosen your deadline (because it’s the editor who determines

the deadline)… But you do you!”

Me: “Praise the good fight fought by me from after (volume 10’s deadline day).”

Good times receiving this Nue-style enigmatic support from Nasu. On that subject, when Nasu said he was going to pull up a whiteboard to explain to all of us why Nue’s Exorcist is so good, I thought that him doing it would make the Nue reference double as a Tsukihime reference, but that’s a topic for another day.

I also had a question for Sanda. I asked: “I’m wanting one more damage dealer for the ElMelloi Classroom, and I’m thinking Yvette’s Mystic Eyes can help me with that. What’s the best Mystic Eye she can make at this point in time?”

Sanda: “High damage… High damage… It’s not exactly a Mystic Eye, but there’s the Mystic Eye… *********…”

Me: “Ah!? …Is that allowed!? Wouldn’t that get a big-name Dead Apostle on her case?” Sanda: “But… You know how El-Melloi II is… He would analyze it during the situation and want to teach it to Yvette, consequences be damned…”

Me: “Yikes, I’m starting to see why everyone is so afraid of the Plundering Duke.”

And that’s how FsF is made, going with the flow and brute-forcing my way through obstacles, but always taking details seriously.

And one more thing that has been taking my attention: the anime! Yes, the anime! The special episode Fate/strange Fake: Whispers of Dawn released in March 2023! A fantastic spectacle with full voice acting and Sawano’s soundtrack! Even I was astonished at the visual feast a serious version of the Hero King can produce…! It was so wonderful that it got me pacing and hollering and dancing on my own as I watched the video—And now they announced the TV run’s production!

We’ll tell you how that’s going to air at a later date, but Morii and I are very involved supervisors, so look forward to further news!
