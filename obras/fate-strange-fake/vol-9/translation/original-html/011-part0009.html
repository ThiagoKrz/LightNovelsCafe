Snowfield Has Vanished

“Snowfield Has Vanished”

One day, one moment— Snowfield City was gone from the face of Earth.

A lodge in the American mountains

A luxurious lodge in a natural Bounded Field deep within the mountains.

At its center there was a conference room where the Snowfield Holy Grail War backers paced nervously.

The personnel responsible for deciding on Operation [Aurora fall]—the deletion of everything in Snowfield area via the strike of a special warhead—continued to be thrown off by the real-time information feed.

“Hurricane Inanna shrank and stationed itself in a point 20km away from Snowfield.”

“Okay… We’re expecting it to vanish when we remove Snowfield’s Greater Grail, but stay vigilant. Release the cover stories to the all press institutions the moment it’s gone. Accept some of the Holy Church’s protests and demands. But do not, I repeat, do not let them interfere any more than necessary.”

“We received a contact from the Clock Tower. They want us to be thorough in identification of the surviving mages.”

“We were already going to be. While Faldeus is extracting the pseudo-core of the Greater Grail, it’s likely to get stolen. Keep him under watch.”

While many reports came from all sectors, the leaders continued to give out precise orders. Until—

“General…!”

Someone yelling the general’s position without saying his name changed the tone of the room.

The general in uniform raised his eyebrow at this man, the chief of the surveillance division.

The man at the conference room’s entrance straightened his posture but was still shaking.

“Snowfield… has… vanished.”

“Hah? What do you mean?”

“Be more clear with your reports.”

“Has surveillance gone offline?”

The people in the room demanded explanations.

If a large mage group was on the move, it was likely they could jam the observation satellites. They demanded a more detailed report to know how to respond, but—

“No, the observation devices in the satellites are operating normally… As far as we know.”

After an inarticulate comment, the chief pressed buttons on the conference room’s console, displaying the images from the surveillance room’s monitors.

“I repeat. Snowfield has… vanished.”

“What am I looking at?”

The general saw it.

All other masterminds of the Holy Grail War saw it.

The abnormality happening in real time in Snowfield.

Everyone gulped, speechless at the satellite pictures in the monitors.

This group, handpicked for their profound understanding of science and magecraft, couldn’t process the abnormality they saw.

They were all frozen, incapable of describing the images until the surveillance chief moved on.

“That’s not all! Hurricane Inanna vanished at the same time. No clouds can be detected.”

“Come again…”

“The countdown to Code 983: [Aurora fall] is still ongoing. How to proceed?”

“Be ready for any situation… We’ll decide at last second whether to carry on.”

With a sour expression, the military commander managed to get the words out of his mouth.

“At this gargantuousness… Is the situation visible by the naked eye observers in Las Vegas?”

“Large-scale concealment magecraft is being employed in the surrounding cities… We put up the fabricated images meant to conceal [Aurora fall]’s execution. It’s possible mages can still see through it.”

“These images are classified under maximum security. If anything leaks, spread similar fake videos and get the uploader dismissed as an ill-tasted edit.”

The satellite images displayed—a giant circle.

A circle so beautiful it must have been drawn with a compass. The area that contained Snowfield was cut off the map and they could see there was the blackest darkness.

And the strangest part was— A circle of a different color at the center of the black circle.

It was impossible to determine what that was from the overhead camera, but the lateral surveillance images from an on ground camera showed it clearly.

A sphere.

A humongous sphere with kilometers in diameter floated about 150m above sea level.

The sphere rotated slowly, but without moving any millimeter away from its position.

It was like a tiny moon suddenly appeared, with an orbit that perfectly matched Earth’s rotation and revolution.

The sphere seemed artificial to its onlookers.

Its base color resembled dry bricks and it was decorated with beautiful blue and gold stones in geometric shapes, resembling lapis lazuli.

Meanwhile, nothing could be observed at the pitch black surface, Snowfields urban center, residential areas, streets, industrial district—and of course, its 800 thousand inhabitants—were nowhere to be found.

The object looked like the giant egg of a god or a fiend, or an unknown planet suddenly floating over Earth.

Was this is a jar for concentrating malisons?

Or a womb about to birth something?

The organizers of this Fake Holy Grail War could do nothing but watch the eventual conclusion.

When something were to out of the sphere, that will be the end of everything.

It’ll be over before anyone can figure if what was being birthed is positive or negative.

The question is whether its demise will also be the world’s demise.

The answer was still unknown to all.

The threads of fate crossing Snowfield’s Holy Grail War converged, heading towards the end of everything.
